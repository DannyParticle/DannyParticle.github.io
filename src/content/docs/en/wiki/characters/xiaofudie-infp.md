Xiaofudie (INFP; the name is a homophone of the nickname "Little Butterfly"; the male version is "Xiao Wangzi" (萧望梓), a homophone of "Little Prince")

Original name: Mediator

Nicknames: Little Butterfly, Nen Die, Crying Cat Head<small> (added on July 25, 2025)</small>

One of the main characters of the [MBTI in Wang Wei's Poems (王维诗里的MBTI)](/wiki/channel/) Channel.

*<small>————Even the loneliest island is embraced again and again by the tide.</small>*<small> (added on July 25, 2025)</small>

## Cognitive functions (based on the Beebe model)

First function (Dominant / Hero): Fi (Introverted Feeling)

Second function (Auxiliary / Parent): Ne (Extraverted Intuition)

Third function (Tertiary / Eternal Child): Si (Introverted Sensing)

Fourth function (Inferior / Anima-Animus): Te (Extraverted Thinking)

Fifth function (Opposing / Opposing Personality): Fe (Extraverted Feeling)

Sixth function (Critical / Senex or Witch): Ni (Introverted Intuition)

Seventh function (Blind Spot / Trickster): Se (Extraverted Sensing)

Eighth function (Devilish / Demon): Ti (Introverted Thinking)

## Easter eggs & trivia

- The length of Xiaofudie's hair shows whether the character has entered or left a relationship (whether friendship, family, or romance); after a breakup, for example, the hair gets cut short. The hair colour was hand-picked by Liulian (ISFP).
- In the book *Who Is Lying? My Truth Notes*, Xiaofudie goes by another name, "Yana".
