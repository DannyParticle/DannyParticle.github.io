*Buqi (不器)* is an animated series on the MBTI in Wang Wei's Poems Channel. Set in the final years of the Republic of China, it tells the story of sixteen element bearers (variants of the 16 type characters) who gather one after another to summon Buqi and fight the Dahuang clan. The title comes from "the gentleman is not a vessel" in *The Analects: Governance*, meaning that a gentleman should not be like a utensil, useful in only one respect.

Director: Wang Yuanyuan (王元元)

Producer: Wiki (维基)

Animation: Wang Yuanyuan (王元元), Taizi (太子), Yuenan (月南), Daidai (戴戴), Damian (大面), Jingshen (惊蜃), Heben (和本), TV, Eol
