Yunzhong Luyinxi (云中鹿饮溪) is female, with a personality type of INFP. In the [MBTI in Wang Wei's Poems (王维诗里的MBTI)](/wiki/channel/) Channel she is the voice actor for [Lü Qiangren (吕强人)](/wiki/characters/lvqiangren-entj/), [Xiaofudie (萧福叠)](/wiki/characters/xiaofudie-infp/), and [Mama (马麻)](/wiki/characters/mama-isfj/) in the daily-life version, and for [Bailidianmo (百里点墨)](/wiki/characters/bailitouhei-infj/) and [Shanjie (单洁)](/wiki/characters/shange-esfj/) in the gender-swapped version.
