MBTI in Wang Wei's Poems (王维诗里的MBTI) is a channel on bilibili (its main platform), Douyin, Xiaohongshu, LOFTER, Weibo, YouTube and other platforms that creates content themed around MBTI (Myers–Briggs Type Indicator), starring the 16 personality figures from the [16personalities](https://www.16personalities.com/ch) website. It is run by [Wang Yuanyuan](/wiki/people/wangyuanyuan/) and [Wiki](/wiki/people/viki/) (both pseudonyms), and its name plays on the meme "xxxx in Wang Wei's poems". Work email: wangweishilidembti@qq.com.

There is a wiki covering [Jungian theory and the Jungian eight functions](/wiki/theory/jung-eight-functions/) that is being verified and improved.

Owing to circumstances beyond our control, maintainers in some parts of mainland China have been unable to access the Fandom wiki reliably (starting in late July 2026), so the Fandom wiki was forked to a GitHub Pages wiki. A contributor has also made a wiki on Moegirlpedia; our thanks to them, [link here](https://mzh.moegirl.org.cn/%E7%8E%8B%E7%BB%B4%E8%AF%97%E9%87%8C%E7%9A%84MBTI). We hope that in the future it will be easier for all of us to contribute to the MBTI in Wang Wei's Poems wiki.

## Some metaphors

- In the episode "The 16 Types' Emotional Needs", Zu'an (ISTP) calls Liu Lian (ISFP) "Lian-ge" (Brother Lian), while Liu Lian's voice is female. This is in fact an allusion to the fact that the default female ISFP image on the [16personalities](https://www.16personalities.com/ch) website is often mistaken for a male one

![The many formulas appearing around Hao Se](/wiki-images/A21828b8ed050768b3dec1cfe2babe7a.webp)

*The many formulas appearing around Hao Se*

- In the episode "How to Cheer Up the 16 Types", in one scene many formulas appear around Hao Se (ISTJ), but most of them are wrong. This is in fact an allusion to the fact that ISTJ's dominant function is Si (Introverted Sensing) rather than Ti (Introverted Thinking) — that is, whatever is remembered is taken as true, regardless of whether it is logical.
- In the episode "The 16 Types Find Out Their MBTI", there is a scene where Xiao Fudie (INFP) and Su Dajian (ENFJ) are guessing other people's MBTI together, and Guo Zhemei (ENTP) disguises himself as an ENFP to make them guess his type. This is in fact an allusion to ENTP's dominant function being Ne (Extraverted Intuition), and to the misjudgments produced when the blind-spot functions Fi (Introverted Feeling) and Fe (Extraverted Feeling) are misused. The "roasted butterfly" storyline in that episode also reflects ENTP's behaviour of shattering INFP's fantasies and emotions, roasting them like a butterfly so that they feel disappointment and even hurt.
- In the episode "The 16 Types' Ideal Type", there are two scenes in which Mo Jing (ESTP) and Liu Lian (ISFP) each list good looks as one of their conditions. This alludes to the relatively strong Se (Extraverted Sensing) of ESTP and ISFP, their keen sense of the "here and now", and their attention to the direct information received through the five senses (sight, hearing, touch, and so on).
- In the "INTJ Character Profile", Guo Zhemei and Shutiao (XNTP) argue with Lü Qiangren and Huzi (XNTJ) about the "tangled mess". The essence lies in the difference between Ti and Te: the former pursues essential logic, the latter pursues efficiency. At the same time, Guo Zhemei's (ENTP) "why is chaos wrong" is a manifestation of negative dialectics.

## Character relationship settings in videos (except where the title specifies a particular relationship)

Apart from videos whose character relationships are fixed by a specific theme, or which have a specific source of inspiration, the relationships in the channel's other videos are decided first by what the video is going to do (the rough relationships) and second by drawing lots to arrange the combinations (who has which relationship with whom). - ENFJ and INTP are brother and sister (inspired by [Wang Yuanyuan](/wiki/people/wangyuanyuan/) and her older brother, and appearing fairly often) (*How Much Trouble Can the 16 Types Cause as Siblings?*)
- The male and female ENFPs are older sister and younger brother (*Shocking! Accidentally Finding Out Your Rival Has a Crush on You!*, February 2, 2024)
- The male and female ESFJs are brother and sister (*The 16 Types Meet a Copycat*, November 8, 2024)
- The male and female ISTJs are brother and sister (*The 16 Types' Reactions to Finding Out Their MBTI*, August 23, 2024)
