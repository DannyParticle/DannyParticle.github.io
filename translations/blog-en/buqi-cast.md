Buqi (不器) is the animated series produced by the channel MBTI in Wang Wei's Poems (王维诗里的MBTI). It tells the story of sixteen element bearers who come together in the final years of the Republic of China to summon Buqi.
Here the character names, personality types, and elements are gathered into a table for easy cross-reference.

| Character (Traditional / Simplified) | Personality type | Element |
|---|---|---|
| Mo Jing (莫競/莫竞) | ESTP | Yellow |
| Ma Qinqin (馬欽欽/马钦钦) | ISFJ | Spirit |
| Amei (阿眉) | ENTP | Willow |
| Su Shijian (蘇世簡/苏世简) | ENFJ | Fox |
| Baili (百裏/百里) | INFJ | White |
| Shan Ge (單歌/单歌) | ESFJ | Dao |
| Hao Wuyu, Hao Wuyan (郝無語、郝無言/郝无语、郝无言) | ISTJ | Grey |
| Yu Cang (雨蒼/雨苍) | ISTP | Earth |
| Liu Lian (柳憐/柳怜) | ISFP | Wood |
| Lü Zhiqiu (呂知秋/吕知秋) | ENTJ | Fire |
| Chi Zi (池梓) | ESTJ | Water |
| Xiao Die (蕭迭/萧迭) | INFP | Spirit |
