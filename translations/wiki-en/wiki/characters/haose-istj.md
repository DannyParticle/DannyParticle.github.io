Haose (ISTJ; the name comes from the nickname "Sese Old Man" (瑟瑟老头) and is a homophone of "lustful" (好色). Gender-swapped name: Hao Yu (郝钰), a homophone of "lustful desire" (好欲).)

Original name: Logistician

Aliases: Robot, Blue Old Man<small> (added on July 25, 2025)</small>

One of the main characters of the [MBTI in Wang Wei's Poems (王维诗里的MBTI)](/wiki/channel/) Channel.

The male and female versions of this character are brother and sister.

*<small>————"Romance can also be a boat at the lakeside that cannot bear to sail away."</small>*<small> (added on July 25, 2025)</small>

## Cognitive functions (based on the Beebe model)

First function (Dominant / Hero): Si (Introverted Sensing)

Second function (Auxiliary / Parent): Te (Extraverted Thinking)

Third function (Tertiary / Eternal Child): Fi (Introverted Feeling)

Fourth function (Inferior / Anima-Animus): Ne (Extraverted Intuition)

Fifth function (Opposing / Opposing Personality): Se (Extraverted Sensing)

Sixth function (Critical / Senex or Witch): Ti (Introverted Thinking)

Seventh function (Blind Spot / Trickster): Fe (Extraverted Feeling)

Eighth function (Devilish / Demon): Ni (Introverted Intuition)

## Easter eggs & trivia

- The ISTJ was named "Haose" because Wang Yuanyuan (王元元) had the impression that the ISTJ character design on 16Personalities "looks lustful".
- Haose and Hao Yu are the only characters who do not regularly use voice actors; for the vast majority of the time they are voiced by AI.<small> (The voice actor is Chengjin (承锦), for the male version.)</small>
- Haose and Hao Yu emit computer system sounds in daily life or when they "crash", which fits the stereotype that ISTJs are "human machines".
- In the old character art, Hao Yu's occupation was a police officer.
