Guozhemei (ENTP; the female version is "Guo Zhemei" (郭哲玫); both names are homophones of the nickname "Eyebrow Fracture")

Original name: Debater

Nicknames: Eyebrow Fracture, Yakoukou Box (Amazon Box), Old Brow<small> (added on July 25, 2025)</small>

One of the main characters of the [MBTI in Wang Wei's Poems (王维诗里的MBTI)](/wiki/channel/) Channel.

*<small>————Stars on the riverbed, ocean currents hanging upside down, an oasis beyond the black hole, time running backwards, humanity walking on clouds, rivers surging towards the mountaintop.</small>*<small> (added on July 25, 2025)</small>

## Cognitive functions (based on the Beebe model)

First function (Dominant / Hero): Ne (Extraverted Intuition)

Second function (Auxiliary / Parent): Ti (Introverted Thinking)

Third function (Tertiary / Eternal Child): Fe (Extraverted Feeling)

Fourth function (Inferior / Anima-Animus): Si (Introverted Sensing)

Fifth function (Opposing / Opposing Personality): Ni (Introverted Intuition)

Sixth function (Critical / Senex or Witch): Te (Extraverted Thinking)

Seventh function (Blind Spot / Trickster): Fi (Introverted Feeling)

Eighth function (Devilish / Demon): Se (Extraverted Sensing)

## Easter eggs & trivia

- In the episode *16 Types as Creator Gods*, the ENTP script was suspected of being "padded", and the studio later explained the reason for this in the comments section: "Using 'abstract, wildly imaginative' forms of expression to present ENTP's storyline was an attempt to break away from the stereotypical impression of ENTP as 'mean and chaotic'."
