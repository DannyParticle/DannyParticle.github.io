Mojing (ESTP; the name is a homophone of "sunglasses". The female version is "Mo Yan", a homophone of "ink eye")

Original name: Entrepreneur

Aliases: Sunglasses, Blind Man<small> (added on July 25, 2025)</small>

One of the main characters of the [MBTI in Wang Wei's Poems (王维诗里的MBTI)](/wiki/channel/) Channel.

*<small>————We are about to ride the wind and rise.</small>*<small> (added on July 25, 2025) (Whether a character was accidentally omitted from "ride the wind and rise" here remains unclear)</small>

## Cognitive functions (based on the Beebe model)

First function (Dominant / Hero): Se (Extraverted Sensing)

Second function (Auxiliary / Parent): Ti (Introverted Thinking)

Third function (Tertiary / Eternal Child): Fe (Extraverted Feeling)

Fourth function (Inferior / Anima-Animus): Ni (Introverted Intuition)

Fifth function (Opposing / Opposing Personality): Si (Introverted Sensing)

Sixth function (Critical / Senex or Witch): Te (Extraverted Thinking)

Seventh function (Blind Spot / Trickster): Fi (Introverted Feeling)

Eighth function (Devilish / Demon): Ne (Extraverted Intuition)

## Easter eggs & trivia

- When Mojing takes off his sunglasses, his 33 eyes are revealed; he is very likely nearsighted. (In the school version and the Buqi (不器) version, Mojing wears glasses or photochromic lenses.)
- Mojing's eyes are blue.
- Mojing's license plate reads "牛BXX6" (in the episode *How the 16 Types Show They're Feeling Down*); it was "ESTP" at first.
