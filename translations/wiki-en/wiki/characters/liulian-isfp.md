Liulian (ISFP; the name is a homophone of the nickname "Durian Head". Gender-swapped name: Liu Ye)

Original name: Adventurer

Aliases: Little Painter, Durian Head<small> (added on July 25, 2025)</small>

One of the main characters of the [MBTI in Wang Wei's Poems (王维诗里的MBTI)](/wiki/channel/) Channel.

*<small>————"I left this sheet of paper blank because I wanted to fill in the little flowers we just picked together."</small>*<small> (added on July 25, 2025)</small>

## Cognitive functions (based on the Beebe model)

First function (Dominant / Hero): Fi (Introverted Feeling)

Second function (Auxiliary / Parent): Se (Extraverted Sensing)

Third function (Tertiary / Eternal Child): Ni (Introverted Intuition)

Fourth function (Inferior / Anima-Animus): Te (Extraverted Thinking)

Fifth function (Opposing / Opposing Personality): Fe (Extraverted Feeling)

Sixth function (Critical / Senex or Witch): Si (Introverted Sensing)

Seventh function (Blind Spot / Trickster): Ne (Extraverted Intuition)

Eighth function (Devilish / Demon): Ti (Introverted Thinking)

## Likes and dislikes

Likes: rebirth fiction

## Easter eggs & trivia

- Liulian cannot look a friend in the eye when he is telling a lie to spare their feelings.
- When Liulian gets angry, his voice turns into a boiling kettle (?).
- In earlier videos Liulian has played a ghost (not a scary one); this claim was later confirmed by a scene in the episode *16 Types as Creator Gods*, where Mama (ISFJ) comes down to earth to watch a horror movie and the character is Liulian. (Uncertain, pending verification.)
