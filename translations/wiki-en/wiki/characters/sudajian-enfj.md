Sudajian (ENFJ; gender-swapped name: Su Daqi (苏达琪). The name comes from a blend of "Su Daji" (苏妲己) and "big sword / big banner" (大剑/大旗).)

Original name: Protagonist

Aliases: Big Treasure Sword, Big Sword, Treasure Sword, Su Dajian<small> (added on July 25, 2025)</small>

One of the main characters of the [MBTI in Wang Wei's Poems (王维诗里的MBTI)](/wiki/channel/) Channel.

*<small>————The branches grow, toward (like) countless pilgrim paths, slowly climbing toward the heavens — that is the devotion of people running toward the beings they love.</small>*<small> (added on July 25, 2025)</small>

## Cognitive functions (based on the Beebe model)

First function (Dominant / Hero): Fe (Extraverted Feeling)

Second function (Auxiliary / Parent): Ni (Introverted Intuition)

Third function (Tertiary / Eternal Child): Se (Extraverted Sensing)

Fourth function (Inferior / Anima-Animus): Ti (Introverted Thinking)

Fifth function (Opposing / Opposing Personality): Fi (Introverted Feeling)

Sixth function (Critical / Senex or Witch): Ne (Extraverted Intuition)

Seventh function (Blind Spot / Trickster): Si (Introverted Sensing)

Eighth function (Devilish / Demon): Te (Extraverted Thinking)

## Easter eggs & trivia

- Sudajian is associated with Su Daji (苏妲己) because the popular impression of ENFJs is that they are "succubi".
